South Korean Government-Funded R&D Initiative  ·  CORNERS Project

Mobile Robotics LiDAR SLAM Human Pose Detection Digital Twin Unity Disaster Response Patent

Project Overview

A South Korean government-funded R&D initiative aimed at developing and demonstrating disaster awareness, prediction, and response technologies to ensure rapid evacuation and user safety in deep underground facilities — such as GTX complex stations and long tunnels under high-risk emergency scenarios including fire, earthquakes, and flooding.

Team Collaboration — Localization & Digital Twin Synchronization

A real-world localization and synchronization test was conducted in a company corridor. In collaboration with a Unity engineer, the corridor environment was reconstructed as a 3D model in Unity and synchronized with a physical mobile robot in real time.

The robot first performed LiDAR SLAM-based mapping to generate a spatial map of the corridor, which was then integrated into Unity to create a corresponding 3D environment. Using localization data, the real robot and its virtual counterpart in Unity were accurately synchronized.

A human pose detection algorithm running on the robot detected nearby people. When a person was detected, their position was accurately estimated and visualized in real time within the Unity 3D environment, enabling precise human-robot spatial awareness.

International Seminar Presentation

Research outcomes were presented at an international seminar, showcasing the system's capabilities for disaster-aware navigation and real-time digital twin synchronization.
