Project Overview

Count every stroke. Nothing else. A deliberately minimal, fully offline golf stroke counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. Count strokes from your wrist while your phone stays in your pocket, or while another golf app (e.g. 18Birdies) is open on the phone. No account, network, backend, or iCloud.

Architecture

Watch tap ──► WatchEvent (UUID, absolute count) ──► transferUserInfo + sendMessage ──► iPhone: dedupe by id ──► RoundStore (atomic save) ──► RoundSnapshot ──► Watch + Live Activity
ComponentResponsibility
RoundStore.swift Single entry point for every mutation. Each change is saved immediately and rolled back on failure, so on-disk state is never half-updated. Notifies observers after each commit (watch sync, Live Activity).
GolfRound / GolfHole (SwiftData) Round to 18 holes with cascade delete. A revision counter is bumped on every change for sync conflict resolution.
PhoneSyncCoordinator.swift Pushes a RoundSnapshot via updateApplicationContext (durable, latest wins) plus sendMessage when reachable. Dedupes watch events by id; the last 300 ids are persisted.
WatchStore.swift / WatchSyncCoordinator.swift Watch-local state persisted to UserDefaults. Events go through transferUserInfo (durable queue, older queued stroke events for the same hole are collapsed) and sendMessage (instant).
RoundActivityController.swift + RoundLiveActivity.swift Keeps exactly one Live Activity in step with the active round (ActivityKit + WidgetKit).

Engineering Notes — Sync Correctness

Built for real golf-course conditions: Bluetooth drops, a locked phone, and the app not running. The phone remains the source of truth while absolute events and revisioned snapshots keep the watch in sync.
  • The watch sends absolute stroke counts, not +1 deltas, so a duplicated or replayed event cannot double-count.
  • Events are idempotent. Duplicates (same UUID) are ignored. A stale event (older timestamp than a newer phone edit on the same hole) is rejected. "Finish hole" on an already-completed hole does nothing, so a hole can never be skipped.
  • After any event, even a rejected one, the phone re-publishes its snapshot so the watch always converges back to the phone's state.
  • Snapshots with an older revision are ignored on the watch. If the watch's local strokes are newer than the phone's, it keeps them and resends.

Feature Tour

Home

Start New Round, Round History, and a "Resume Current Round" card with a circular 18-hole progress ring and "Started 1:05 PM · Thru 7 · +2". The card has custom swipe-to-delete (short swipe reveals the trash button, long swipe deletes, Mail-style). Starting a new round while one is active asks whether to resume or replace; the replaced round is still saved to history.

Active round

Big hole number; Thru / Score / Total pills; a Par 3 / 4 / 5 selector; and a large 260-pt circular stroke button with haptics (disabled with "SELECT PAR" until par is chosen). A live "2 over par" / "At par" line sits with Undo and Finish Hole buttons. After hole 18, a round-complete screen shows total and score relative to par, followed by a Finish Round confirmation.

Scorecard

Front 9 / Back 9 sections with Out/In subtotals and a total row. Color-coded +/- badges (red under par, blue over par). Tapping a completed hole opens an edit sheet to fix its par or strokes, and totals recalculate. Accessibility labels read scores by name (Birdie, Bogey, Eagle…).

Round History

Completed rounds, newest first, showing date, start time, holes played, duration, total strokes and +/- par. Swipe to delete. Tap a round to see its full scorecard with a summary banner.

Apple Watch

The same big-button counter, sized relative to the watch screen so it fits every case size, with Undo and Finish Hole buttons and haptics. Shows "Hole 5 complete — Set up Hole 6 on your iPhone" until par is picked on the phone. Keeps counting when the phone is unreachable and offers a "Sync now" button.

Live Activity / Dynamic Island

A Lock Screen banner and Dynamic Island (compact, expanded and minimal views) showing the current hole, par, strokes and "Thru n · total · +/-". It updates after every change, including watch taps while the iPhone app is in the background, and dismisses itself 15 minutes after the round finishes.

Light and dark mode

Custom "fairway green" theme; every color adapts to light and dark mode.
