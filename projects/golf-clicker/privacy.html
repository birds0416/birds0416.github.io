Golf Clicker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Golf Clicker is a simple stroke counter for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: Golf Clicker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

Golf Clicker stores the golf data you enter — rounds, holes, and stroke counts — so you can review your rounds later. This data is saved only on your own devices (your iPhone and, if you use it, your Apple Watch). It is never sent to us or to any third party.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required, and none is created.
  • No name, email address, location, health, or fitness data is collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking software is included in the app.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and does not use any third-party services.

iPhone and Apple Watch synchronization

When you use the Apple Watch app, your current round is synchronized directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch using Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. This transfer happens only between your own paired devices and does not pass through any server operated by us.

Live Activities

While a round is in progress, the app can show the current hole and stroke count on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This information is displayed on your device only and is not transmitted anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

Your rounds remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device. Your data may also be included in any device backup you have enabled (such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup); those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings, not by Golf Clicker.

Children's privacy

Golf Clicker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.
