Korea Fire Institute (KFI)  ·  Government R&D Project

Fire Safety R&D Robotics Embedded Systems Government Project

Project Overview

A government-funded research and development project in collaboration with the Korea Fire Institute (KFI), focused on the practical application and field validation of advanced fire suppression supplies and equipment, integrating robotic systems for enhanced firefighting effectiveness and personnel safety.
